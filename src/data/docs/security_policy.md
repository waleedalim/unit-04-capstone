# Company Security Policy

## Access Control
All employees must use multi-factor authentication (MFA) for access to
company systems, including email, VPN, and internal applications. Access
to production systems is granted on a least-privilege basis and reviewed
quarterly by the Security team.

## Data Classification
Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted.
Confidential and Restricted data must be encrypted at rest (AES-256) and
in transit (TLS 1.2+). Restricted data may not be stored on personal
devices or transferred outside approved company systems.

## Incident Response
Any suspected security incident (phishing, malware, unauthorized access)
must be reported to security@company.com within one hour of discovery.
The Security team follows a documented incident response runbook with
four phases: containment, eradication, recovery, and post-incident review.

## Password Requirements
Passwords must be at least 12 characters, rotated every 180 days, and
may not be reused across the last 10 passwords. Password managers are
provided to all employees and are strongly encouraged.

## Vendor Security
Third-party vendors with access to company data must complete a security
questionnaire and sign a data processing agreement before onboarding.
