# Customer Complaint Handling

## Intake
Customer complaints can arrive via email, phone, chat, or the support
portal. Every complaint is logged as a ticket in the support system
within 30 minutes of receipt, tagged by category (billing, product
defect, service quality, delivery, other).

## Triage and Response SLAs
- Critical (service outage, safety issue): acknowledge within 1 hour,
  resolve or provide a mitigation plan within 24 hours.
- High (billing dispute, major defect): acknowledge within 4 hours,
  resolve within 3 business days.
- Standard (general feedback, minor issue): acknowledge within 1
  business day, resolve within 5 business days.

## Escalation
If a customer is not satisfied with the initial resolution, the ticket
is escalated to a team lead. Escalations involving refunds over $500 or
legal/safety concerns are routed directly to the Customer Success
Director.

## Root Cause and Follow-up
For product defects, the support team files a linked engineering ticket
and follows up with the customer once a fix ships. Complaint trends are
reviewed monthly by the Customer Success team to identify systemic
issues and inform product and policy changes.

## Customer Satisfaction Policy
Every closed ticket is followed by a satisfaction survey. Customer
Success aims to keep the resolution satisfaction score above 90%.
